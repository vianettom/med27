Stay Where It Happens

We Look Forward To Seeing You at Medicarians 2027!

Medicarians 2027 will take place at the Caesars Forum in Las Vegas. Medicarians attendees will have access to a special room rate at the host hotels.

Medicarians does not share or sell attendee data. Anyone soliciting you to book your hotel is not authorized or affiliated with Medicarians and is likely a scam. All attendees are encouraged to book through the room block link provided below. Please contact a member of the Medicarians team with any questions.

Plan Your Travel

Give Yourself the Full Show.

  • Arrive on Sunday, April 11th

    For the best show experience, we recommend arriving Sunday evening, April 11th or early Monday, April 12th to make sure you catch the Kick-Off Day Summits and Official Kick-Off Reception on Monday.

  • Depart on Thursday, April 15th

    For the complete experience, depart Thursday morning, April 15th so you don't miss the Official After Party!
