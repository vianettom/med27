The Audience

Who Should Attend & Why.

The entire senior health & wealth distribution industry under one roof.

  • Producers

    Agents & Brokers

    • Learn directly from major carriers, health plans, and regulators about the forces shaping our industry.
    • Get expert guidance from the most successful and forward-thinking producers and agency owners in the country.
    • Network with 4,000+ like-minded players in the Medicare, ACA, and senior wealth distribution landscape.

  • Distribution

    Agency Owners & Principals

    • Discover how fellow agency owners are navigating a complex and competitive market and staying efficient and nimble with new technology.
    • Learn how to scale your agency without compromising service to clients.
    • Accelerate your growth by developing new relationships with FMOs, IMOs, carriers, health plans, and agents.

  • Plans

    IMOs & FMOs

    • Learn how to optimize your business at scale, working with thousands of independent agents and agencies.
    • See how successful FMOs and IMOs holistically comply with regulations and stay ahead of emerging trends.
    • Solidify new partnerships with carriers and health plans as a competitive advantage.

  • Plans

    Carriers & Health Plans

    • Hear the latest product design trends across the entire distribution ecosystem– from senior health to wealth offerings.
    • Learn directly from agents & brokers about what seniors want and how to differentiate your products.
    • Meet new distributors and strengthen your relationship with current ones.

  • Capital

    Investors & Startups

    • Connect with startup leaders and discover emerging companies that are disrupting the marketplace.
    • Decipher how regulatory changes are guiding investment decisions.
    • Meet other investors, venture capital, and private equity teams who are accelerating innovation in our industry.

  • Compliance

    Regulators & Compliance Executives

    • Understand the current market challenges and opportunities that leaders are facing.
    • Convey evolving regulatory frameworks, and communicate compliance requirements for an efficient marketplace.
